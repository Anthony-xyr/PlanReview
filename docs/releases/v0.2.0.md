## 中文

这一版的重点是：AI 速记能记住你的写法，界面换了新配色并支持深色模式，类别可以自己管理，另外修了几个影响使用的问题。

### ✨ 新功能

- **速记习惯学习**：AI 拆速记时理解错了，可以在确认卡片上点「纠正」，写一句哪里不对，AI 会重新拆，并提出一条习惯（比如「句首的『月.日』是布置日期，不是截止」）。点「确认保存」时才会记下，以后解析速记都会照着用。习惯在 设置 → AI → 速记习惯 里查看、改写、停用或删除，最多 30 条。
- **深色模式**：设置 → 外观，可选跟随系统、浅色、深色，点一下立即生效。时间轴、周复盘图表、对话框、悬浮窗都适配了深色。
- **类别管理**：侧栏新增「类别」页，按类别查看还没结束的任务，点开就能修改全部属性。「管理类别」里可以新建、改名、换色、调整顺序、删除和恢复类别；删除时可以选择把没结束的任务改到别的类别。AI 速记只会从现有类别里选。
- **分场景的 AI 推理强度**：速记解析、课表识别、帮我排时间、周复盘生成、周复盘追问，可以分别设成 Low / Medium / High（模型支持 `reasoning_effort` 时生效）。简单的拆分求快，复盘想得更深。
- **逾期提示**：已过截止日期的任务标红，显示「已过截止」，待办等页面顶部会提示有几项已过截止。

### 💄 改进

- **新配色**：颜色更中性，边框更淡，按钮以描边为主，每个页面只保留一个最主要的实心按钮。
- **侧栏分组**：下半部分分成「复盘」和「工具」两组，和上面的页面导航区分开。
- **悬浮窗**：速记框平时收起成一行快捷键提示，按快捷键或点一下才展开，回车保存后自动收起，Esc 取消。主窗口侧栏新增「显示悬浮窗」，关掉悬浮窗后可以从这里再打开。
- **周复盘**：打开时停在最近一个有记录的周，‹ › 翻页会跳过空周；只完成或新建了任务、没有计时的周也会显示统计，不再是一整页空白。
- 界面上的「大类」统一改称「类别」。

### 🐛 修复

- **中文 PDF 课表读不出字**：教务系统导出的、使用 STSong 等中文字体的 PDF，以前会被当成扫描件，发给 AI 的只有表格线框。现在能正确读出文字。（[#3](https://github.com/Anthony-xyr/PlanReview/issues/3)，由 [@ya2yo](https://github.com/ya2yo) 在 [#2](https://github.com/Anthony-xyr/PlanReview/pull/2) 中修复）
- **点设置对话框的滚动条会关掉对话框**，改到一半的设置也会丢失。（[#1](https://github.com/Anthony-xyr/PlanReview/issues/1)，由 [@ya2yo](https://github.com/ya2yo) 报告）
- **对话框关掉后再也打不开**：快速连按两次 Esc 关掉设置后，再点「设置」没有反应。
- **悬浮窗变高后缩不回去**：关掉 AI 确认卡片后，悬浮窗现在会恢复原来的大小。
- **速记解析经常超时**：等待时间从 30 秒放宽到 60 秒。

### 📦 安装

1. 下载下方的 `PlanReview-Setup-0.2.0.exe` 并运行。
2. 已经装了 0.1.0 的话，直接安装覆盖即可，**数据会保留**（数据库在 `%APPDATA%\plan-review\plan-review.db`）。
3. 安装包没有代码签名，首次运行时 Windows SmartScreen 会提示「未知发布者」，点「更多信息」→「仍要运行」。

### ⚠️ 已知问题

- 悬浮窗还没有磨砂效果，目前是半透明纯色，后续版本再做。

## English

This release lets AI capture learn how you write, adds a new color scheme with dark mode, gives you full control over categories, and fixes several bugs that got in the way.

### ✨ New

- **Capture habits**: When AI misreads a quick note, click **纠正 (Correct)** on the confirm card and say what's wrong. AI re-parses it and proposes a habit (for example, "a leading `month.day` is the date it was assigned, not the due date"). The habit is saved only when you click **确认保存 (Save)**, and every later capture follows it. Review, edit, disable or delete habits in Settings → AI → 速记习惯 (Capture habits), up to 30.
- **Dark mode**: Settings → 外观 (Appearance): follow the system, light, or dark. It applies immediately. The timeline, weekly charts, dialogs and the floating window all support it.
- **Category management**: A new **类别 (Categories)** page shows open tasks by category, and each task opens with all of its fields editable. In **管理类别 (Manage categories)** you can add, rename, recolor, reorder, delete and restore categories. When you delete one, you can move its open tasks to another category. AI capture only picks from existing categories.
- **Per-task AI reasoning effort**: Set Low / Medium / High separately for capture parsing, timetable import, day planning, weekly review and weekly review follow-up questions. This takes effect when the model supports `reasoning_effort`. Keep quick parsing fast and let reviews think harder.
- **Overdue markers**: Tasks past their due date are shown in red as overdue, and task pages show how many are overdue at the top.

### 💄 Improvements

- **New color scheme**: More neutral colors and lighter borders. Buttons are mostly outlined, and each page keeps only one solid primary button.
- **Grouped sidebar**: The lower part is split into **复盘 (Review)** and **工具 (Tools)**, separate from the page navigation above.
- **Floating window**: The capture box collapses to a one-line shortcut hint and only expands on the hotkey or a click. It collapses again after saving with Enter; Esc cancels. The main window sidebar gains **显示悬浮窗 (Show floating window)**, so you can bring it back after closing it.
- **Weekly review**: Opens on the most recent week that has records, and ‹ › skip empty weeks. A week with only completed or new tasks and no logged time now shows its stats instead of a blank page.
- "大类" is renamed "类别" (category) everywhere in the UI.

### 🐛 Fixes

- **Chinese PDF timetables imported with no text**: PDFs exported by university systems with Chinese fonts such as STSong were treated as scans, and AI only received the table lines. Their text is now read correctly. ([#3](https://github.com/Anthony-xyr/PlanReview/issues/3), fixed by [@ya2yo](https://github.com/ya2yo) in [#2](https://github.com/Anthony-xyr/PlanReview/pull/2))
- **Clicking the settings dialog's scrollbar closed it**, losing unsaved changes. ([#1](https://github.com/Anthony-xyr/PlanReview/issues/1), reported by [@ya2yo](https://github.com/ya2yo))
- **Dialogs could not be reopened**: After closing Settings with a quick double Esc, clicking Settings again did nothing.
- **The floating window stayed tall**: It now shrinks back after the AI confirm card closes.
- **Capture parsing often timed out**: The timeout is raised from 30 to 60 seconds.

### 📦 Install

1. Download `PlanReview-Setup-0.2.0.exe` below and run it.
2. If 0.1.0 is installed, just install over it. **Your data is kept** (the database is at `%APPDATA%\plan-review\plan-review.db`).
3. The installer is not code-signed. On first run, Windows SmartScreen shows "Unknown publisher"; click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

### ⚠️ Known issues

- The floating window doesn't have a frosted blur yet; it's a translucent solid color for now. This is planned for a later version.
